# Known Limitations

- PDFs must contain searchable text. Image-only scans need OCR before matches can be detected; OCR is not included.
- The Runtime does not automatically understand arbitrary document formats or layouts.
- Results depend on the trusted signed Pack that you import and how its checks fit your documents.
- Community Runtime `0.5.0-beta.1` targets Windows x64 and Debian amd64. Release assets are staged, but downloads are not public until separate authorization; beta terms and third-party licensing review remain pending.
- Some document layouts may require a separately scoped Custom Trace Pack.
- Review processed PDFs and evidence before relying on them. Outputs may be incomplete or incorrect for a particular document.
- Trace Pro does not provide legal, accounting, compliance, academic, or other professional or regulatory certification.
- The Windows beta executable may show a Microsoft SmartScreen warning because it is not code-signed.
